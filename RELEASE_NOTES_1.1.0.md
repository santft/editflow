## What's new in 1.1.0

### Find the person faster
- **Who to edit**: type a name (e.g. `Megan Fox clips`) and press **Search**. EDITFLOW finds the
  videos, you pick the person, and the edit starts.
- **One person picker for all your videos**, filled in as each one is ready. The faces seen from the
  most angles come first.
- **Photos for still pictures**: when the TikTok shows photos and you added none, EDITFLOW searches
  the web for photos of the person and keeps only the ones where your person is found by face
  (Settings → *Photos for still-image slots*). Topics have a **Search the web for photos** button too.

### Copies more of the TikTok
- **Zooms in and out**: measured clip by clip and copied with the same amount, the same speed and
  the same aim, toward your person's face when the TikTok zooms on a face.
- **Black & white** is found reliably: tinted greys, black & white stretches and whole black & white
  edits.
- **Photos the editor zooms into** are recognised as stills.
- **Pictures that don't fill the screen**: a window previewing the next shot, and boxes popping in
  over the clip, are copied.
- **Effects have names** (Flash, Dip to black, Zoom out, Window…), each in its own colour on the
  timeline. Only the kinds EDITFLOW copies reliably are switched on; the others are listed by
  name, ready to switch on.

### Your choices
- **Frame**: keep 9:16, or choose the TikTok's own frame, 1:1 or 4:5 (*Look → Frame*).
- **Colour looks previewed** on the TikTok before you add one: point at a look to see it big.
- A **?** next to every threshold explains what it does.

### Faster
- The TikTok **plays as soon as it's downloaded**; its cuts and effects fill in a few seconds later.
- Finding the effects is 30 to 40% faster, and drawing them on your clips about 4 times faster.
- The clip search learns where the person is in each video (in the background) and passes over
  the parts without them: about half the slow checks.

### Fixes
- No more freeze for up to a minute after an edit is made.
- The sound starts every time and stays in sync, even when clicking quickly; dragging the playhead
  while the video plays works smoothly.
- Zoomed clips no longer drift from side to side.
- A slot no clip fits (too long for any shot of the person) gets a still of the person instead of
  stopping the edit.
- Messages look like the rest of the app, and *Save to the library?* no longer covers the result.
- An end credit read in pieces ("EDITED BY …") is never copied onto your clips.

### Removed
- The optional Google Gemini step. EDITFLOW now uses no AI service: everything is measured on your PC.
